1. What Exactly is a Prop Firm?

A Proprietary Trading Firm (Prop Firm) is a company that provides traders with simulated or real capital to trade financial markets (Forex, Indices, Commodities, Crypto). Instead of risking your own money, you pay a one-time fee to take an Evaluation Challenge. If you hit the profit target without breaching the drawdown rules (maximum allowed loss), you become a "Funded Trader".

2. The Evaluation Phase: Rules to Know

Most firms have a 1-step or 2-step evaluation process. You must adhere to strict risk management protocols:

  • Profit Target: Usually between 8% to 10% on the first phase.
  • Daily Drawdown: The maximum you can lose in a single day (often 4% or 5%).
  • Maximum Drawdown: The total absolute or trailing loss allowed on the account (usually 8% to 10%).
